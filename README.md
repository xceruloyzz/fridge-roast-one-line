Entry for the [Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh](https://make.fun/c/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) bounty on make.fun — one line, at most 200 characters, roasting a smart fridge.

## The entry

> My smart fridge downloaded a firmware update, rebooted for an hour, and now refuses to chill anything until I accept its new terms of service.

142 characters. `main.py` writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) — directly to the final file, no temp files, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
